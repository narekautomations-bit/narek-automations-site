Sample Projects

Illustrative examples of the kind of work I build

These are representative examples to show scope and approach — not results from a specific client engagement.

Illustrative example Excel · VBA

Month-End Close Workbook

A CPA firm's staff manually copied numbers from 6 client sub-ledgers into a master close file every month. The rebuilt workbook pulls each source automatically, applies standard adjusting entries, and highlights any account that doesn't tie to prior month within a set tolerance.

A multi-day manual roll-up becomes a same-day review.
Illustrative example Excel · Python

Cash Reconciliation Tool

A small business matched a bank statement export against internal records by eye, line by line. The tool auto-matches on amount, date window, and reference text, then produces a short exceptions list of only what still needs a human look.

Hundreds of lines matched automatically; only true exceptions need review.
Illustrative example Excel · Office Scripts

Invoice & AR Tracker

Overdue client invoices were tracked in someone's memory and a scattered inbox. The new tracker ages every open invoice automatically and generates a ready-to-send follow-up list every Monday morning.

Nothing falls through the cracks — the sheet does the chasing.
Illustrative example Word · PDF · Python

Mail-Merge / PDF Renamer

Engagement letters were built one at a time in Word, printed to PDF, and renamed by hand for 80+ clients each season. The new script merges the template against a client list and outputs correctly-named, ready-to-send PDFs in one run.

A week of tedious admin work becomes a single click.
