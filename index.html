Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know before reaching out

answers.sh

$ explain --cost

Every project is scoped individually after a free consultation, and you'll always get a fixed quote in writing before any work starts — reach out for a custom quote based on what you need.

$ explain --timeline

It depends on scope, but most single-workflow automations (a close workbook, a reconciliation tool, a mail-merge script) are delivered in 1-3 weeks after the proposal is approved.

$ explain --access

Usually not directly — most projects are built and tested against sanitized sample or historical files, so you control exactly what's shared and when.

$ explain --confidentiality

A confidentiality agreement can be signed before any files are shared, and your data is never reused, stored beyond project needs, or shared with anyone else.

$ explain --tooling

Mainly Excel (formulas, VBA, Office Scripts), Word, and PDF generation, often glued together with Python or AI-assisted scripting when it's the better tool for the job. No new software for your team to learn unless you want one.

$ explain --support

Yes — every project includes a short training walkthrough and a post-delivery support window, and ongoing support or future enhancements can be arranged after that.
